- Sovereign Identity: Cryptographic DIDs backed by 12/24-word BIP-39 recovery phrases stored on your device.

- Zero-Algorithm Control: Subscribe to verified topic channels (#Tech, #AI, #Music, #Art, #Crypto, #Philosophy) or explore live streams, reels, and community feeds without shadowbans.
- Creator Economy & Wallet: Direct 95/5 creator tipping, CosmWasm CW20 community tokens, and sovereign escrow marketplace.
