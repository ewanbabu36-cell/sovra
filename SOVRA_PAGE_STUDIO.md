# SOVRA Page Studio

## 1. Overview
The **Page Studio** provides identity and audience management for verified organizations, businesses, and public figures. Unlike personal profiles or broadcast channels, Pages represent institutional identities with customizable calls-to-action (CTA) and customer lead capture.

---

## 2. Page Studio Capabilities

### 2.1 Overview
- **Persisted Metrics**: Real follower count (`followersCount`), published dispatches, and incoming customer inquiries.
- **Brand Identity**: Verified banner, organization badge, and decentralized DID binding.

### 2.2 Content Management
- **Brand Posts**: Updates, articles, product launches, and media drops published under the Page persona.
- **CTA Conversion**: Direct links to Send Message, Visit Website, Book Service, or Tip Sovereign Node.

### 2.3 Community & Team Roles
- **Multi-Admin Operations**: Businesses can delegate operational responsibilities to team members (`ADMIN`, `EDITOR`, `MODERATOR`).
- **Permissions Enforced**: Only the `OWNER` can reassign administrative control or delete the Page.

### 2.4 Page Settings
- **Business Profile**: Bio, contact email, physical or mesh location, and operating hours.
- **Call-to-Action Action**: Dynamic CTA configuration (`message`, `website`, `book`, `tip`).
- **Discoverability**: Toggle listing in the mesh business directory.
