## Production Verification Schema:
$$\text{UI} \to \text{Client Logic} \to \text{API} \to \text{Auth} \to \text{AuthZ} \to \text{Business Logic} \to \text{Persistence} \to \text{Storage} \to \text{Events} \to \text{Notifications} \to \text{Multi-user} \to \text{Refresh} \to \text{Restart} \to \text{Mobile} \to \text{Tests}$$

### Status Classifications:
- **VERIFIED**: Fully implemented, durable on disk, authenticated, verified across multi-user journeys and restarts.
- **PARTIALLY VERIFIED**: Implemented and passing tests, but hardware-gated or awaiting physical handset bench verification.
- **BROKEN**: Fails functional assertion or causes state corruption.
- **SIMULATED**: Relies on synthetic timer, dummy adapter, or mock responses.
- **DISCONNECTED**: Service exists in package but not called by runtime daemon.
- **NOT IMPLEMENTED**: Feature not yet built.
